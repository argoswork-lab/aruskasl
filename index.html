The product

One agent for the question every owner asks: "how much cash do we actually have?"

SMBs run money across bank accounts, accounting apps, and marketplace payouts that never talk to each other. ArusKas unifies them into a live cash ledger — then goes to work.

Live cash position

Real-time balance across every account and channel. One number you can trust, in Rupiah, updated as money moves.

13-week forecast

Rolling cash forecast built from your actual inflows, payables, and payroll cycles — not a spreadsheet you forget to update.

Anomaly alerts

Catch overspend the week it happens. ArusKas flags unusual payouts, margin drift, and silent cost creep before they compound.

Invoice collection agent

Overdue invoice? ArusKas drafts the reminder and sends it on WhatsApp — politely, persistently, with your approval first.

How it works

Ten minutes to your first real cash number.

01

Connect

Link your accounting tool (Jurnal, Accurate, Xero), upload bank statements, or drop in a CSV. ArusKas reconciles everything into one live cash ledger — no migration, no finance team needed.

02

Understand

Ask anything in Bahasa or English — "kenapa margin turun minggu ini?" — and get an answer backed by your actual transactions, with the drivers and the chart to prove it.

03

Act

ArusKas doesn't stop at insight. It drafts the supplier payment plan, sends the invoice reminder on WhatsApp, and delivers your morning brief — always with your approval before money or messages move.

CLAUDE-NATIVE

Finance data is messy. We chose the model that reads it best.

ArusKas is built Claude-first — long-context reasoning over entire transaction ledgers, reliable tool use for multi-step finance workflows, and careful writing for every customer-facing message. Not model-agnostic. Optimized.
